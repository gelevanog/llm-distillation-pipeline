Router: student first, teacher on fallback

Share answered by the studentRouted exact match (gold)

Hover the chart for exact values. At threshold 0 the student answers everything it can parse; at 1 the teacher answers everything.

Policy

The student answers first. Its answer goes to the teacher when the JSON does not parse, fails the schema, or its confidence (the lowest first-token probability over the five structured fields) is below 0.77.

Threshold: calibrated on val (teacher labels), 51 tickets, target exact match 80% on the answers the student keeps.
